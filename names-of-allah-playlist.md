## 📖 Chronological Lesson-by-Lesson Breakdown

---

### 🔹 Introduction | The Supreme Name
#### Arabic Notation
> # **اللَّه**
> **Transliteration:** *Allāh*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Greatest Name; The Only One Worthy of All Worship.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Ustadh Hisham Abu Yusuf opens the series by establishing that **Allāh** is the foundational proper name of the Creator around which all other attributes orbit. It encompasses all divine perfections and signifies the Supreme Deity to whom all hearts incline with love, awe, and total devotion.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 01 | The Master & Nurturer
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الرَّبّ**
> **Transliteration:** *Ar-Rabb*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Lord, Master, Owner, Nurturer, and Sustainer.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
The term *Ar-Rabb* combines ownership, authority, and nurturing care (*Tarbiyah*). Ustadh Hisham highlights how Allah nurtures His creation step-by-step—both physically (providing air, food, health) and spiritually (guiding through revelation and trials)—leading believers toward spiritual maturity and complete reliance (*Tawakkul*).

---

### 🔹 Lesson 02 | The Living & The Maintainer
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْحَيُّ • الْقَيُّومُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Ḥayy, Al-Qayyūm*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Ever-Living • The Self-Sustaining & Maintainer of All Creation.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
These two names are considered by many scholars to constitute the *Ism Allāh al-Aʻẓam* (The Greatest Name). Ustadh Hisham explains that **Al-Ḥayy** possesses complete, eternal life without beginning or end, unaffected by slumber or death, while **Al-Qayyūm** maintains the existence of every atom in the cosmos without relying on any creation.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 03 | The Most Merciful
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الرَّحْمَٰنُ • الرَّحِيمُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Ar-Raḥmān, Ar-Raḥīm*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The All-Compassionate / Most Gracious • The Extremely Merciful.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
The lecture explores the vastness of divine mercy. **Ar-Raḥmān** describes an intrinsic, vast mercy that encompasses every creature in existence (believer and non-believer alike), whereas **Ar-Raḥīm** refers to the specific, tailored mercy bestowed upon the believers in this life and the Hereafter.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 04 | The Forgiving & Pardoning
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْغَفَّارُ • الْغَفُورُ • الْعَفُوُّ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Ghaffār, Al-Ghafūr, Al-ʻAfuww*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Continual Forgiver • The All-Forgiving • The Supreme Pardoner.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Ustadh Hisham contrasts the nuances of divine forgiveness:
- **Al-Ghafūr / Al-Ghaffār**: Veil and cover the sin repeatedly so it is not exposed, protecting the servant from punishment.
- **Al-ʻAfuww**: Goes even deeper—completely erasing the record of the sin as though it never happened, removing all guilt and consequence.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 05 | The Sovereign & Owner
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْمَلِكُ • الْمَالِكُ • الْمَلِيكُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Malik, Al-Mālik, Al-Malīk*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Sovereign King • The Owner of All • The Absolute Ruler.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
This session addresses true authority and dominion. While human kings possess temporary, imperfect rule over tiny parcels of land, Allah is **Al-Malik** whose sovereignty over the heavens and earth is absolute, unchallengeable, and unaffected by time or revolt.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 06 | The Most Knowledgeable
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْعَلِيمُ • الْعَالِمُ • الْخَبِيرُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-ʻAlīm, Al-ʻĀlim, Al-Khabīr*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The All-Knowing • The Knower of Everything • The All-Aware / Intricately Acquainted.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Ustadh Hisham emphasizes that Allah’s knowledge spans the past, present, future, and even what *could have been*. **Al-Khabīr** adds the dimension of subtle, inner knowledge—knowing the hidden motives of the heart, the unspoken whispers, and the exact weight of every intention.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 07 | The Watchful Protector
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْحَفِيظُ • الرَّقِيبُ • الْمُهَيْمِنُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Ḥafīẓ, Ar-Raqīb, Al-Muhaymin*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Preserver / Guardian • The All-Watchful • The Supreme Overseer.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
This lesson cultivates *Muraqabah* (mindfulness of God). Allah is **Al-Ḥafīẓ**, preserving the deeds of mankind and protecting the cosmos from falling into chaos; **Ar-Raqīb**, watching over every action with uninterrupted attention; and **Al-Muhaymin**, guarding and controlling all outcomes.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 08 | The All-Powerful
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْقَادِرُ • الْقَدِيرُ • الْمُقْتَدِرُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Qādir, Al-Qadīr, Al-Muqtadir*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Able • The All-Powerful • The Omnipotent Master of Power.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
The speaker illustrates Allah's effortless omnipotence. His power is complete and free from fatigue or difficulty. When Allah decrees a matter, He simply says *"Kun"* (Be), and it comes into being (*Fayakūn*), giving the believer total confidence during times of helplessness.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 09 | The Protective Guardian
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْوَلِيُّ • الْمَوْلَىٰ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Walī, Al-Mawlā*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Protecting Friend / Guardian • The Supreme Ally & Helper.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Allah is the special **Walī** (close friend and ally) to the righteous believers. Ustadh Hisham notes how Allah takes His allies out of spiritual darkness into light, defending them against harm and providing comfort when worldly allies fail.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 10 | The Creator & Fashioner
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْخَالِقُ • الْخَلاَّقُ • الْبَارِئُ • الْمُصَوِّرُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Khāliq, Al-Khallāq, Al-Bāriʼ, Al-Muṣawwir*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Creator • The Continuous Creator • The Originator • The Fashioner of Forms.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
This lecture traces the stages of divine craftsmanship:
1. **Al-Khāliq**: Conceives and measures creation out of nothingness.
2. **Al-Bāriʼ**: Brings the created entity smoothly into reality.
3. **Al-Muṣawwir**: Shapes and details each creature with unique, flawless design and individuality.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 11 | The Independent
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْغَنِيُّ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Ghaniyy*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Self-Sufficient • The Absolutely Independent.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Ustadh Hisham reminds viewers that Allah needs no worship, food, helper, or companion. If all of humanity were to disbelieve, it would not decrease His dominion by an atom; conversely, our obedience benefits only ourselves.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 12 | The Undefeatable & All-Wise
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْعَزِيزُ • الْحَكِيمُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-ʻAzīz, Al-Ḥakīm*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Undefeatable / All-Mighty • The All-Wise.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
A frequent Quranic pairing. Power without wisdom leads to tyranny, while wisdom without power leads to weakness. Allah joins **Al-ʻAzīz** (unconquerable strength) with **Al-Ḥakīm** (perfect judgment), ensuring every divine action is executed with flawless purpose and timing.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 13 | The Ultimate Provider
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الرَّزَّاقُ • الرَّازِقُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Ar-Razzāq, Ar-Rāziq*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Ultimate Provider • The Granter of Sustenance.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Ustadh Hisham clarifies that *Rizq* (provision) goes beyond monetary wealth. Allah as **Ar-Razzāq** provides physical sustenance (food, shelter, health) as well as spiritual provision (beneficial knowledge, faith, wisdom, and peace of heart).

---

### 🔹 Lesson 14 | The Subtle & All-Aware
#### Arabic Notation
> # **اللَّطِيفُ • الْخَبِيرُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Laṭīf, Al-Khabīr*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Subtle / Most Gentle • The All-Aware.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
**Al-Laṭīf** delivers kindness, safety, and blessings in subtle ways that pass completely unnoticed by human eyes until their fruition. He guides affairs behind the scenes with gentle precision while remaining fully cognizant (**Al-Khabīr**) of every tiny nuance.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 15 | The Powerful & Firm
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْقَوِيُّ • الْمَتِينُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Qawiyy, Al-Matīn*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The All-Strong • The Firm / Possessor of Unyielding Strength.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
These attributes describe strength that never diminishes. **Al-Qawiyy** possesses complete ability to accomplish His will, and **Al-Matīn** signifies firmness that never tires, wavers, or experiences stress under any circumstance.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 16 | The Real (Al-Haqq)
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْحَقُّ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Ḥaqq*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Real • The Absolute Truth • The Ever-True.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Allah is the ultimate reality in a world of fleeting illusions. His essence is truth, His speech is truth, His promises are true, and the Day of Judgment is indisputably true. Recognizing **Al-Ḥaqq** anchors the human soul in certainty.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 17 | The Near & Responsive
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْقَرِيبُ • الْمُجِيبُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Qarīb, Al-Mujīb*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Ever-Near • The Responsive / Answerer of Prayers.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
A deeply comforting lesson on prayer (*Duʻāʼ*). Allah is closer to His servant than their own jugular vein. He is **Al-Mujīb**, personally responding to every sincere call—either by granting what was asked, deflecting harm, or saving a greater reward for the Hereafter.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 18 | The Guide
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْهَادِي**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Hādī*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Guide • The Granter of Guidance.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Ustadh Hisham discusses how Allah guides creatures instinctively to survive, and guides human hearts to faith, moral clarity, and salvation (*Hidāyah*). Guidance is entirely in His hands, making our frequent prayer *"Ihdinā-ṣ-Ṣirāṭa-l-Mustaqīm"* essential.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 19 | The Appreciative & Tolerant
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الشَّكُورُ • الشَّاكِرُ • الْحَلِيمُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Ash-Shakūr, Ash-Shākir, Al-Ḥalīm*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Most Appreciative • The Rewarder of Good • The Forbearing.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Allah as **Ash-Shakūr** appreciates the minor good deeds of His servants, multiplying their rewards exponentially. As **Al-Ḥalīm**, He does not rush to punish sinners, granting ample time for repentance and reflection out of immense forbearance.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 20 | The Gift-Giver & Opener
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْوَهَّابُ • الْفَتَّاحُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Wahhāb, Al-Fattāḥ*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Supreme Gift-Giver • The Opener of Doors & Ultimate Judge.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
**Al-Wahhāb** bestows gifts (*Hibah*) unconditionally without expecting anything in return. **Al-Fattāḥ** opens doors of mercy, understanding, sustenance, and victory when all human avenues appear tightly closed.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 21 | The Limitless in Favor
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْمَنَّانُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Mannān*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Bestower of Limitless Favor • The Gracious Giver.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
This name emphasizes Allah initiating immense blessings upon His creation prior to any merit or asking on their part. His greatest favor (*Mannah*) upon humanity is sending the Prophet ﷺ and guiding us to Islam.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 22 | The Perfect & Source of Peace
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْقُدُّوسُ • السَّلاَمُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Quddūs, As-Salām*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Absolutely Pure / Sacred • The Source of Peace & Perfection.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
- **Al-Quddūs**: Transcends every defect, error, or human limitation.
- **As-Salām**: Is Himself pure perfection and the sole source of safety, security, and peace in this life and Paradise (*Dār as-Salām*).

---

### 🔹 Lesson 23 | The Generous, Honorable & Majestic
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْكَرِيمُ • الأَكْرَمُ • ذُو الْجَلاَلِ وَالإِكْرَامِ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Karīm, Al-Akram, Dhūl-Jalāli wal-Ikrām*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Most Generous • The Most Bountiful • Possessor of Majesty and Honor.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Allah’s generosity is boundless. He gives before being asked, pardons when disobeyed, and treats His servants with noble honor. Combining majesty (*Jalāl*) with honor (*Ikrām*) produces revered love in the heart of the seeker.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 24 | The Most Loving
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْوَدُودُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Wadūd*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Most Loving • The Affectionate.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Derived from *Wudd* (active, expressed love). Ustadh Hisham points out that Allah not only loves His righteous servants (*Ḥubb*), but actively manifests affection (*Wudd*) toward them through blessings, acceptance among people, and comforting presence.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 25 | The Greatest & Supreme
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْعَظِيمُ • الْكَبِيرُ • الْمُتَكَبِّرُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-ʻAẓīm, Al-Kabīr, Al-Mutakabbir*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Supreme • The Most Great • The Rightfully Majestic in Pride.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
These names establish Allah’s infinite grandeur. Human minds cannot comprehend His magnificence. **Al-Mutakabbir** denotes that pride and grandeur belong rightfully to Him alone; for creation, pride is a flaw, but for the Creator, it is truth.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 26 | The Majestic & Most High
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْعَلِيُّ • الأَعْلَىٰ • الْمُتَعَالِ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-ʻAliyy, Al-Aʻlā, Al-Mutaʻāl*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The High • The Most High • The Exalted Above All.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
These names confirm Allah's highness across all three dimensions:
1. **Highness of Status (*ʻUluww al-Qadr*)**: Perfect attributes without flaw.
2. **Highness of Power (*ʻUluww al-Qahr*)**: Subduing all creation.
3. **Highness of Essence (*ʻUluww adh-Dhāt*)**: Exalted above the heavens, distinct from creation.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 27 | The Compeller & Restorer
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْجَبَّارُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Jabbār*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Compeller • The Restorer / Mender of Hearts.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
A powerful dual concept explained by Ustadh Hisham:
- **For the arrogant/tyrants**: He compels them and enforces His decree irresistibly.
- **For the broken/weak**: He mend their spirits (*Jabr*), heals their grief, and restores their strength.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 28 | The Giver of Security
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الْمُؤْمِنُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Muʼmin*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The Giver of Security & Faith • The Granter of Safety.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Allah is **Al-Muʼmin** because He confirms the truth of His Messengers with miracles, keeps His promises faithful, and guarantees believers safety from oppression in this life and safety from punishment on Judgment Day.

---

### 🔹 Lesson 29 | The Eternal, One & Encompassing
#### Arabic Notation
> # **الأَوَّلُ • الآخِرُ • الظَّاهِرُ • الْبَاطِنُ • الأَحَدُ • الصَّمَدُ**
> **Transliteration:** *Al-Awwal, Al-Ākhir, Aẓ-Ẓāhir, Al-Bāṭin, Al-Aḥad, Aṣ-Ṣamad*  
> **Primary Meaning:** The First • The Last • The Manifest • The Hidden • The One & Only • The Eternal Refuge.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
This comprehensive lesson synthesizes Allah’s temporal and spatial encompassment:
- **Al-Awwal & Al-Ākhir**: Nothing existed before Him; nothing remains after Him.
- **Aẓ-Ẓāhir & Al-Bāṭin**: Above everything in dominance/evidence; closer than all else in subtlety.
- **Al-Aḥad & Aṣ-Ṣamad**: Absolutely singular, peerless, and sought by all creation for every need while needing none.

#### 🎙️ Speaker Insights & Commentary
Ustadh Hisham concludes the series with a practical roadmap based on the Hadith *"Allah has ninety-nine names... whoever enumerates/comprehends them (*Aḥṣāhā*) will enter Paradise."* True *Iḥṣāʼ* involves:
1. **Knowledge (*ʻIlm*)**: Memorizing and understanding their meanings.
2. **Supplication (*Duʻāʼ*)**: Calling upon Allah by the specific name matching one's need.
3. **Action (*ʻAmal*)**: Internalizing character traits (e.g., showing mercy because Allah is Ar-Raḥīm, acting generously because He is Al-Karīm).
